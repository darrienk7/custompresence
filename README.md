# Custom Presence

Make your Discord activity your own. Choose **Playing**, **Listening**, **Watching**, or **Competing**, add a name, and pick an image or GIF for your activity card. No Discord account token or developer setup is needed.

## Set your activity

1. Open Discord desktop and enable activity sharing in Discord's settings.
2. Open Custom Presence and choose an **Activity type**.
3. Enter an **Activity** name, or leave it blank to show just the activity type.
4. Under **Image / GIF**, choose **Upload** or **Paste public link**. Use **Check link** to check a pasted image.
5. Click **Apply activity** and keep both apps running.

**Advanced options** opens categories for activity text, artwork, timers, parties, buttons, and other settings. Choose a category, make your changes, then apply the activity. **All options** returns to the categories; **Done** returns to the main screen.

Click **Clear** to remove your activity. Closing Custom Presence also clears it. Your last applied settings are remembered for next time.

## Images and GIFs

Uploads accept PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, and AVIF files up to 8 MB. If uploading is unavailable, choose **Paste public link**. Links must use public HTTPS addresses. Giphy, Tenor, and single-image Imgur links are supported; albums and videos are not.

Uploaded images are public. Keep the removal link shown after uploading if you want to delete the image later. Choosing the same file again reuses its existing upload when possible. Clearing your activity or closing the app leaves the hosted image online.

Uploads use ImgBB and are subject to its [Terms](https://imgbb.com/tos), including its 18+ eligibility requirement. Hosting does not guarantee that every image or GIF frame is moderated.

## Help

- **Missing activity:** Keep Discord desktop and Custom Presence open, enable activity sharing, and click **Apply activity** again.
- **Missing party count:** Enter both the current and maximum sizes, apply, and open your expanded Discord activity card.
- **Different preview:** The preview is approximate. Discord controls the final layout, animation, and button visibility.
- **Reset settings:** Open **Advanced options → Saved data** to reset saved settings or forget remembered uploads. Save any removal links first; these controls do not delete hosted images.
